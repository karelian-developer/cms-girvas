**The GIRVAS Content and Site Management System** is a multi-profile **free** open source content and site management system** from **[Karelian Developer Enterprise](https://xn----7sbbafuqffehcie7cvgcl5a9h7d.xn--p1ai/)**. GIRVAS is universal and is designed to work with sites of any complexity and direction.

Please note that **following the recommendation** the wizard-installer system will be installed correctly. In case of technical problems during the installation of the system, please [create an incident](https://gitflic.ru/project/garbalo/cms-girvas/issue) on the official repository page of the system, after which it will monitor the developer. We also suggest that you familiarize yourself with the current [installation guide](https://doc.garbalo.com/projects/cms-girvas/installation.pdf).

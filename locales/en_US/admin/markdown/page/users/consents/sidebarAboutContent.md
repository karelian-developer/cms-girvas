**"Consents"** — the register of personal data subjects' consents. Records consents linked to a document, its version, and locale.

**What you see:**
- **Who** gave consent (login).
- **Which** document and **version**.
- **When**, **from where** (IP), **via what** (User-Agent).
- Whether it was **revoked** and **by whom** (subject / admin).

**Actions:**
- **Revoke** consent on behalf of the user (with a mandatory reason).
- **Export** all consents to **CSV** for audit.

**Purpose:** 152-FZ compliance (art. 9), consent audit, responses to Roskomnadzor requests.

**The GIRVAS content management system** is a multilingual, multi-profile, free, open-source content management system from the [**Karelian Developer**](https://xn----7sbbafuqffehcie7cvgcl5a9h7d.xn--p1ai/) enterprise, aimed at: creating an optimized website in terms of the speed of loading its content, creating a website of any direction with multilingual content with the possibility of their subsequent indexing by search engines. "GIRVAS" began to be developed in 2021 by individual entrepreneur [**Andrey Shestakov**](https://vk.ru/drelagas). The management system is written in the general-purpose programming language PHP **without the use** of third-party frameworks - this is a completely proprietary solution of Karelian production.

### Included in the Register of Russian Software

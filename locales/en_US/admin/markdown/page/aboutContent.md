**CMS «GIRVAS»** is a multilingual, multi-profile free open source content management system from Karelian developers Garbalo, aimed at creating an optimized web resource in terms of the speed of loading its content, creating a web resource of any direction with the ability to publish content in different languages ​​with the possibility of subsequent indexing by search engines. The system began to be developed in 2021 by individual entrepreneur Andrey Shestakov. The management system is written in the general-purpose programming language PHP without the use of third-party frameworks. Based on its own engine.

### Included in the Register of Russian Software
![Emblem of the Ministry of Digital Development](/images/admin/page/about/mincifr_64.png) Based on the [minutes](https://reestr.digital.gov.ru/upload/iblock/05a/oc3uwhy113fcqclvgk5v7ur754dx4l5n/%D0%9F%D1%80%D0%BE%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%20%E2%84%96%20820%D0%BF%D1%80.pdf) of the meeting of the expert council on software of the **Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media of the Russian Federation**, the CMS software "GIRVAS" was included in the register of domestic software dated November 27, 2024.

Наличие CMS «GIRVAS» в данном реестре подтверждает то, что предприятию-разработчику, а также самому программному обеспечению можно доверять. Разработчик делает все необходимое, чтобы улучшать работу своего продукта, поэтому система часто получает различные улучшающие обновления.

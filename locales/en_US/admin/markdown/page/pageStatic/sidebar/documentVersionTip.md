Specify the document version in **X.Y** format (e.g. `1.0`, `1.1`, `2.0`).

When saving, a **new version** will be created. Previous versions are kept in history and available at:

`/page/<page-name>?version=<version>`

Users giving consent (during registration or form submission) will consent to **this exact version**.

> **Important:** for legal documents, you **cannot** change the version without changes to the text. If the text hasn't changed — a new version is not created.
